Nets

Net Central creates nets over APRS.

Participants can coordinate messaging, status, location and other information across everyone in the net.

The Net Monitor provides a console for viewing and controlling nets, their participants and information.

Nets can be started immediately or scheduled for the future, and when secured, can provide reports for archiving.

Stations

Net Central listens to APRS traffic across one or more communication connections, providing situational awareness of stations heard.

Stations, objects and callsigns can be viewed in Explorer, allowing for filtering and messaging with stations.
