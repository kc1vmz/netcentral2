Description of task

Ask a numbered question of a running net and collect answers by participant callsign. Use Net Monitor to ask questions, record answers received through another channel, and review responses. Checked-in participants can also ask and answer questions through APRS messages.

Questions and answers remain associated with the net and are available in its archive and Q&A Report after completion.

Prerequisites

  • For browser controls, sign in as the net owner or an administrator authorized to manage the net.
  • Select a running net. A scheduled definition or archived net cannot accept new questions or answers.
  • Have the intended participants check in. An answer can only be recorded for a currently checked-in callsign.
  • Verify that the net's selected communication instances are available and allow transmission, and that destinations have eligible routes.
  • Prepare a concise question and know the correct question number when entering an answer.

Steps to accomplish

  1. Open Net Monitor and select the intended Running net in the carousel. Check its name and callsign.

  2. In Questions, select Ask question. Enter the question text without a radio-command prefix. The browser field accepts up to 500 characters, but short questions are easier to deliver and read over APRS.

  3. Select Ask participants, review the confirmation dialog, and confirm. Net Central assigns the next question number and sends the question to the currently checked-in participants. Select Cancel if you want to leave the question unsent.

  4. Select the question in the independently scrollable Questions list. Entries show their number, such as Q 1. The Answers section displays responses for the selected question.

  5. To record a response received by voice or another channel, select Record answer in the Answers section. Enter the Question number, Participant callsign, and Answer. Use the checked-in callsign, including its SSID when applicable.

  6. Review all three fields and select Save answer. This saves immediately without a second confirmation. Select Close to leave the dialog, then select the question to review the recorded response.

Using a radio or APRS client

After checking in, address these APRS messages to the running net's callsign:

CommandExampleResult
Q textQ Can you operate until 1800?Creates a numbered question and sends it to checked-in participants.
A number textA 1 Yes, available until 1800Records your answer to question 1.
QLQLReturns questions you have not yet answered.
QR numberQR 1Returns question 1 and its recorded answers.

Use the actual question number from this net. Net Central replies with a thank-you message after accepting a radio question or answer.

Verifying success

  1. Confirm the new question appears once in Questions with the expected text and number.
  2. Have a checked-in participant verify reception of the numbered question on their APRS client. A question appearing in the browser proves it was recorded, not that every transmission reached its destination.
  3. After a response, select the question and check that Answers shows the correct callsign and text. The answer also produces a recorded event in the net's message history.
  4. From a participant's radio, use QR with the question number to retrieve the recorded responses. QL should no longer include a question that participant has answered.
  5. If updates are paused, resume them to see incoming responses. Confirm that you selected the correct question before treating an empty Answers section as a missing response.

Additional considerations

  • One answer per callsign: Recording another answer from the same callsign to the same question replaces its previous answer. Review the callsign and question number before saving a correction.
  • Answer delivery: Answers are stored for review; they are not automatically broadcast to every participant. Use the console or QR to review the responses.
  • Question scope: Numbers belong to a specific net. Question 1 in one net is unrelated to question 1 in another net or a later scheduled occurrence.
  • Length: Browser question and answer fields accept up to 500 characters, but APRS messages have shorter transport limits and include command or question-number prefixes. Keep operational text brief rather than assuming a long browser entry will arrive intact in one radio message.
  • Late arrivals: The initial question transmission targets participants checked in at that time. A participant who checks in later can request unanswered questions using QL. The net's Question reminder option can also be managed through Edit Net.
  • Missing responses: Check participation, the question number, route availability, and communication status. A station must be checked in when its answer is recorded.
  • Archives: After the net is secured, open Archives, select the net, and review Questions and Answers or choose Generate Q&A Report. Archived records are read-only.
