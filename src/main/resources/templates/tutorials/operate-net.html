Description of task

Participate in a running net using APRS radio commands: report status and equipment, exchange messages, ask and answer questions, find other participants, and check out when finished.

Send these commands as APRS message text addressed to the net callsign. They operate on the net, not on a local radio's command prompt. Each command reference below links directly to its entry in Help.

Prerequisites

  • Know the running net's callsign and have a working APRS path to Net Central.
  • Check in using CI and continue using the same station callsign and SSID. See Checking into a net for admission and check-in instructions.
  • Use an APRS radio or client that can address and send text messages and display replies. A web account is not required for radio participation.
  • Keep the net's operating instructions handy, including any agreed meanings for Red, Yellow, and Green status.

Steps to accomplish

  1. Set your APRS message destination to the running net's callsign. If you have not joined, send CI and confirm acceptance.

  2. Send H for radio help, I for net information, or L for the active participant list.

  3. Report your status and equipment using the examples below. Replace example values with your own information and send the message to the net.

  4. Exchange messages and answer questions as needed. Keep free-text commands in separate messages so later command words are not accidentally included in their text.

  5. Review replies and, if available, your participant entry in Net Monitor. Correct errors with another appropriate command rather than repeatedly resending an uncertain request.

  6. When finished, send CO. Wait for the checkout reply. This ends your participation, not the whole net.

Status and equipment

Command and exampleEffect
STG / STY / STRSet your status color to Green, Yellow, or Red. These are separate from descriptive status text.
S Ready at stagingSet your descriptive status to Ready at staging.
T STAGINGSet a single-word tactical callsign.
F 146.520Report your monitored voice frequency; this does not tune your radio.
X 25Report transmit power in watts.
E BATTERYReport your primary power source.
B GENERATORReport backup power.
Y MOBILEReport your radio style.

Consult the linked E, B, and Y help entries for supported values. Short, fixed-value commands can be combined, for example STG X 25 E BATTERY Y MOBILE. Send S and other free-text commands separately because they consume the remaining message text.

Messages, questions, and coordination

Command and exampleEffect
M Supplies have arrivedRecord a message and forward it to other checked-in participants; it is not echoed back to its sender.
MC Please contact me by voiceRecord a message for the Net Control console without forwarding it to the group.
R 5Request replay of the last five recorded incoming net messages.
Q Can you remain until 1800?Create a numbered question and send it to checked-in participants.
QLRequest questions you have not answered.
A 1 Yes, until 1800Record your answer to question 1. A later answer to that same question replaces your earlier answer.
QR 1Request question 1 and its recorded answers.
P N1ABC-7Send a ping notice to that checked-in participant. This is not a guarantee of a response.
O N1ABC-7Request the last known station status, or Unknown when unavailable.
INV N1ABC-7Invite a station when participant invitations are enabled. An invitation adds the station to the expected list but does not check it in.
NCIToggle notifications of participation changes. Sending it again reverses the setting.

For fuller examples, see Send messages to a net and Questions and Answers in a net. The Radio Commands help also contains separate Net Manager and object command groups.

Verifying success

  1. Review the command reply for acceptance or a specific error. An APRS protocol acknowledgment alone does not prove the requested operation was accepted.
  2. Use L to verify active participation and color letters: (G), (Y), (R), and (U) for Unknown when applicable. Status text and equipment can be reviewed in Net Monitor's Participant details.
  3. For group messages, ask another participant to verify reception. For questions, use QR with the correct number to review answers; QL should omit questions you have answered.
  4. After CO, verify the reply says you checked out. The console retains your participation history and sets your color to White/Unknown, while the active participant list no longer includes you.

Additional considerations

  • Command scope: Always confirm the message destination. A command sent to Net Manager or an object can have different semantics from the same command sent to a net.
  • Length: Keep the complete APRS message, including command and arguments, within your client's supported message limit. Use single-word arguments for T, F, X, E, B, and Y.
  • Free text: S, M, MC, Q, and A include text after the command. Do not append unrelated commands to that text. For A and QR, use a question number belonging to the selected net.
  • Privacy: MC is hidden from non-managing users' console message views, but the APRS transmission itself is not private.
  • Routes: Pings, invitations, and forwarded messages depend on eligible routes and available transmission-enabled connections. A station listed as Expected may not yet have a known route.
  • Checkout and rejoining: CO sets White/Unknown. There is no STW command. Sending CI again rejoins with Green, subject to the net's current admission rules.
  • Net completion: Securing or automatically ending a net ends participation and preserves the last stored colors. You cannot continue operating in the archived net.
  • Position: Status and equipment commands do not send coordinates. Transmit a position from your APRS client if you want the net map to show your station.
